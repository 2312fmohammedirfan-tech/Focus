01. Understanding the User

We conducted surveys and interviews with 5 participants to identify core frustrations with existing task apps.

🔍

The Problem

Users feel overwhelmed by complicated categorization and "notification fatigue" from existing tools.

📊

The Data

75% of surveyed students forget assignments because of poor reminder systems in current apps.

💡

The Solution

A minimalist interface focusing on quick task creation and smart, non-intrusive reminders.

02. User Personas

Arjun

College Student, 20

Goal

Organize assignments and deadlines without feeling overwhelmed.

Pain Points

  • Forgetting tasks due to clutter
  • Too many notifications
  • Complicated workflows

Priya

Working Professional, 28

Goal

Track daily work tasks and balance them with personal errands.

Pain Points

  • Poor reminder systems
  • Difficult task categorization
  • Lack of dark mode
